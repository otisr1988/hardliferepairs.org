Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers to common repair questions. If yours is not here, ask Kodi directly.

ATVs & Off-Road

My ATV just clicks when I try to start it. What is wrong? +
A single loud click usually means a bad starter solenoid. Multiple rapid clicks usually point to a weak or dead battery. Start by checking the battery voltage with a multimeter — should read 12.6V or higher when fully charged. If the battery is good, test the solenoid. See the ATV Starter Solenoid Guide.
How do I know if my ATV battery is bad versus the solenoid? +
Test the battery voltage first. A fully charged 12V battery should read 12.6V or more at rest. If it reads below 12V, charge it and test again. If voltage is fine but the ATV still clicks, the solenoid or starter motor is the likely culprit.
What does a solenoid replacement cost? +
A replacement starter solenoid costs $15–30 on Amazon. The repair takes 20 minutes and requires only basic hand tools. A shop would charge $80–150 for the same job.

Home Appliances

My dryer runs but does not heat up. What should I check first? +
Check the thermal fuse first — it is the most common cause and costs $5–10 to replace. Test it with a multimeter on continuity mode. If it has blown, also check that the exhaust vent is not blocked (blocked vents overheat the fuse). See the full Dryer Not Heating Guide.
Can I fix a dryer myself without any experience? +
Yes. The most common dryer repairs — thermal fuse, heating element, thermostat — require only a screwdriver and a multimeter. No prior appliance experience needed. The full repair guide walks through every step with clear instructions.
Does this guide work on my brand of dryer? +
The dryer guide covers Whirlpool, Kenmore, Maytag, Samsung, and GE models. Most electric dryers share the same basic design. The exact part locations vary slightly, but the diagnostic process is the same.

Small Engines & Chainsaws

How do I know when a spark plug needs replacing? +
Replace it if: the engine is hard to start, runs rough, or loses power. Visually, a bad plug looks black and sooty (rich mixture), white or blistered (too lean/hot), or has a cracked porcelain insulator. Most small engines should get a new plug every 100 hours or once per season. They cost $3–8.
How often should I replace a chainsaw chain? +
Replace when the chain no longer cuts efficiently even after sharpening, has visible damage to links, or when you have sharpened it so many times the cutters are too short. A dull chain causes kickback and puts stress on the motor. See the Chain Replacement Guide.

Tools & Electrical

What tools do I need to start doing DIY repairs? +
Start with these essentials:
  • Digital multimeter (auto-ranging, $40–60)
  • Socket set — 1/4" and 3/8" drive with SAE and metric
  • Phillips and flathead screwdrivers (multiple sizes)
  • Combination wrenches
  • Needle-nose pliers
This covers 90% of the repairs on this site. Check the tool store for specific recommendations.
Do I need an expensive multimeter? +
No. A $40–60 auto-ranging multimeter handles every test covered in the guides — voltage, continuity, resistance. You do not need a $200 Fluke for basic repair work. The Klein MM420 is a reliable pick in that range.
Is it safe to test a live electrical outlet with a multimeter? +
Yes, when done correctly. Hold the probes by the plastic handles only, never touch the metal tips while the probes are in a live outlet, and use a meter rated CAT III for household wiring. Never test with both hands — keep one hand in your pocket to prevent current passing through your chest. See the Multimeter Guide for step-by-step instructions.
