# HardLife Repairs & Home DIY

A professional DIY repair website featuring guides, tools, and resources for small engine repair, welding, electrical work, and home improvement projects.

## Live Website
https://hardliferepairs.org

## Pages

- **index.html** - Homepage with services overview and featured products
- **guides.html** - DIY repair guides listing page
- **guides/atv-starter-solenoid.html** - ATV starter solenoid replacement guide
- **guides/dryer-not-heating.html** - Dryer heating element repair guide
- **guides/how-to-use-multimeter.html** - Multimeter beginner guide
- **store.html** - Amazon affiliate product store
- **about.html** - About the business and mission
- **contact.html** - Contact form and social links
- **privacy.html** - Privacy policy (required by law)
- **affiliate-disclosure.html** - Amazon Associates disclosure (required)
- **sitemap.xml** - SEO sitemap for Google
- **robots.txt** - Instructions for search engine crawlers
